## Controlling heavy or life-threatening bleeding
Apply steady, firm, direct pressure over the wound using a clean dressing, gauze, or the cleanest available cloth (a t-shirt is acceptable if nothing else is on hand). If you have disposable gloves or a plastic bag, use them to avoid direct contact with the blood. Hold continuous pressure — do not repeatedly lift the dressing to check the wound, since this can restart bleeding. If blood soaks through, add more dressing on top of the first layer rather than removing it.
Source: American Red Cross, "Bleeding (Life-Threatening External)" — https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/bleeding-life-threatening-external

## When direct pressure is not enough
For severe, life-threatening bleeding from a limb that does not stop with direct pressure, a tourniquet can be used, but only by someone trained to apply one correctly, placed above the wound (between the wound and the heart) and tightened until bleeding stops. If you are not trained and no trained help is available, continue firm direct pressure and call emergency services immediately. For severe bleeding from areas a tourniquet cannot be applied to (such as the scalp, neck, shoulder, groin, or back), wound packing by a trained person is the alternative — otherwise, maintain firm direct pressure and get emergency help.
Source: American Red Cross, "Bleeding (Life-Threatening External)" — https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/bleeding-life-threatening-external

## Getting emergency help for bleeding
Call emergency services immediately for any bleeding that is heavy, spurting, does not slow with direct pressure, or if the person shows signs of shock (see the shock guidance). While waiting for help, keep applying direct pressure and keep the person as still and calm as possible. Do not remove any object that is embedded in a wound — stabilize it in place and seek emergency care instead of pulling it out.
Source: American Red Cross, "Bleeding (Life-Threatening External)" — https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/bleeding-life-threatening-external
