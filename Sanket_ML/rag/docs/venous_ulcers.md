## Venous (leg) ulcers - general care
Summary: Keep a venous ulcer clean and covered, and get a clinician to assess it.
- Keep the wound clean and covered as a clinician has advised.
- If a healthcare provider has told you to, raise the leg above heart level when resting to reduce swelling.
- Do not put on compression bandages yourself unless you are trained - the wrong compression can be harmful.
- Get it assessed by a clinician, especially if it is getting bigger, more painful or looks infected.
Source: General first-aid-level guidance on venous ulcer care consistent with the wound-care references in this corpus (infection signs in minor_wound_care.md apply here too).
