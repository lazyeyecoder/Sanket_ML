## Pressure ulcers (bed sores) - general care
Summary: Take pressure off the area and tell a nurse or doctor.
- Take pressure off the area - help the person change position so they aren't lying or sitting on it.
- Keep the skin around it clean and dry; pat dry, don't rub.
- Keep bedding or seating smooth, without creases.
- Tell a nurse or doctor promptly about any pressure sore, or skin that is red or darker and doesn't turn pale when pressed.
Source: NHS-affiliated pressure ulcer patient guidance — https://www.leedsth.nhs.uk/patients/resources/bed-sores-pressure-sores-and-pressure-ulcers/ ; https://www.cntw.nhs.uk/resources/pressure-ulcers-patient-information-leaflet
