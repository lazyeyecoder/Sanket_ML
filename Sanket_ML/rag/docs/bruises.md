## Treating a bruise
Summary: Rest and raise the bruised area and hold a wrapped cold pack on it.
- Rest the area and raise and support the injured part in a comfortable position.
- Hold a cold pack (or a bag of frozen vegetables) wrapped in a cloth or tea towel on the bruise for up to 20 minutes at a time - never put ice straight on the skin.
- If the injury is recent (last 24 to 48 hours), raise the limb above the level of the heart if you can.
Source: St John Ambulance, "Bruises" — https://www.sja.org.uk/first-aid-advice/bruise/ ; NHS patient guidance, "Ice and heat treatment" — https://www.gloshospitals.nhs.uk/your-visit/patient-information-leaflets/ice-and-heat-treatment/

## When a bruise needs medical attention
Summary: See a clinician if a bruise lasts over 3 weeks or pain and swelling get worse.
- Get medical attention if a bruise has not gone after 3 weeks.
- Get medical advice if the pain increases, the swelling keeps growing, or the area becomes hot to touch.
- Older people and people taking blood-thinning medicine bruise more easily - get medical advice if you are worried.
