## Recognizing shock
Summary: Shock shows as pale, cold, clammy skin, fast breathing and confusion.
- Watch for signs of shock: restlessness or irritability, confusion or drowsiness, pale or ashen cool moist skin, nausea or vomiting, fast breathing and pulse, and extreme thirst.
Source: American Red Cross, "Shock" — https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/shock

## First aid for shock
Summary: Call emergency services, lay the person flat, keep them warm and watch them.
- Call emergency services immediately.
- Control any external bleeding first.
- Lay the person flat on their back. If there is no sign of a head, neck, back or leg injury, raise their feet about 6 to 12 inches (15 to 30 cm).
- Cover them with a blanket to keep them warm, but do not overheat them.
- Do not give food or water.
- Keep watching their alertness and breathing until help arrives.
Source: American Red Cross, "Shock" — https://www.redcross.org/take-a-class/resources/learn-first-aid/shock
