## Diabetic foot/skin ulcers - general care
Summary: Keep a diabetic ulcer clean and get it checked by a clinician promptly.
- Clean the ulcer gently with soap and water, unless a clinician has told you to use something else, and pat it dry.
- Do not soak the wound in a bath or whirlpool, and do not use hydrogen peroxide.
- Do not cut away dead tissue yourself - only a trained clinician should do that.
- Get it checked by a clinician promptly: a diabetic wound can get worse without much pain, especially if it hasn't improved in about a month or shows signs of infection.
Source: Consistent with Mayo Clinic diabetic foot ulcer care guidance — https://connect.mayoclinic.org/discussion/diabetic-foot-ulcers/ ; WebMD "How to Care for Diabetic Ulcers and Sores" — https://www.webmd.com/diabetes/diabetes-sores-ulcers-care
